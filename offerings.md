# Offerings

## Overview

Meditsiin 4.0 provides a comprehensive suite of digitalization solutions for GP centers (Family Centers) in Estonia. Our offerings are designed to enable remote work capabilities, modernize infrastructure, and streamline operations.

---

## Core Offerings

### 1. Hardware Solutions

**Description:** Essential hardware equipment for remote medical work

**Components:**
- Laptops and computing devices
- Headphones and communication equipment
- Essential hardware for remote medical work

**Delivery Model:**
- **Initial Phase:** Act as a middleman between hardware rental companies and customers, providing hardware rental services for medical centers
- **Long-term:** Own proprietary hardware solutions specifically designed for medical centers, transitioning from middleman to direct hardware provider

**Target Users:** All medical staff (doctors, nurses, administrators)

### 10. Blood Pressure Reporting Solution (Long-term: 2-3 years)

**Description:** Patient-facing telemedicine solution that enables remote blood pressure monitoring through image capture and automated data extraction

**Components:**
- Mobile app or web interface for patients to capture blood pressure readings
- Image recognition technology to extract readings from blood pressure monitor displays
- Support for various blood pressure monitor types and models
- Automated data extraction and validation
- Secure data transmission to medical center systems
- Doctor dashboard with table and graph visualization of patient blood pressure data
- Historical trend analysis and reporting
- Alert system for abnormal readings
- Integration with patient records

**Value Proposition:**
- Enables remote patient monitoring without requiring patients to manually enter data
- Reduces errors from manual data entry
- Provides doctors with comprehensive, visual blood pressure tracking
- Supports chronic disease management and remote care
- Improves patient engagement in their own health monitoring
- Enables proactive care based on trend analysis
- Reduces need for in-person visits for routine monitoring

**Key Features:**
- Image capture and OCR technology for blood pressure monitors
- Automatic data extraction from various monitor displays
- Table view showing all readings with timestamps
- Graph visualization (line charts, trends over time)
- Historical data tracking and comparison
- Alert notifications for doctors on abnormal readings
- Patient-friendly interface for easy photo capture
- GDPR-compliant data handling and storage
- Integration with existing medical software systems

**Target Users:** Patients (for submitting readings), Doctors (for viewing and analyzing data), Medical centers (for remote patient monitoring)
