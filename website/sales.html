Our Core Offerings

1. Hardware Solutions

Essential hardware equipment for remote medical work including laptops, computing devices, and communication equipment.

2. IT Infrastructure & Support

Professional system administration and IT infrastructure management. We address the critical gap of non-existent sysadmins in GP centers.

  • 24/7 support for critical medical operations
  • Integration with existing medical software systems
  • Reliable, secure IT infrastructure

3. Medical Software Update Management

Proactive management and updating of medical sector-specific software to ensure latest versions are available and installed.

  • Automated monitoring of software updates
  • Planned update schedules around medical center operations
  • Testing and validation before deployment
  • Reduces security risks from outdated software

4. Communication Tools

Secure, GDPR-compliant communication platforms for team and patient interactions.

  • Internal chat services
  • Call services for patients and teams
  • Email services
  • Support for remote and hybrid work models

5. Productivity Tools

Office suite and collaboration tools that replace fragmented software solutions.

  • Seamless document sharing and collaboration
  • Document management
  • Remote work workflow support

6. Workforce Organization Tools

Comprehensive workforce planning and management system replacing Excel-based planning.

  • Shift planning and scheduling
  • Vacation and sick-leave management
  • Nurse-to-doctor ratio optimization
  • Real-time visibility into team composition

7. Organizational Structure & Design

Strategic organizational structure design for medical centers facing professional shortages.

  • Work planning optimization for limited resources
  • Role definition and responsibility mapping
  • Team structure recommendations
  • Workflow design to maximize efficiency

8. Satisfaction Tracking & Workload Monitoring

Comprehensive system for tracking patient satisfaction and monitoring workload to prevent overwhelm.
