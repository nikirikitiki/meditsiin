Our Offerings

Comprehensive digitalization solutions for GP centers in Estonia

Meditsiin 4.0 provides a comprehensive suite of digitalization solutions designed to enable remote work capabilities, modernize infrastructure, and streamline operations for GP centers (Family Centers) in Estonia.

Core Offerings

1

Hardware Solutions

Essential hardware equipment for remote medical work

Components

  • Laptops and computing devices
  • Headphones and communication equipment
  • Essential hardware for remote medical work

Delivery Model

  • Initial Phase: Act as a middleman between hardware rental companies and customers
  • Long-term: Own proprietary hardware solutions specifically designed for medical centers
2

IT Infrastructure & Support

Professional system administration and IT infrastructure management

Components

  • System administration services
  • IT infrastructure management
  • Technical support and maintenance
  • Integration with existing medical software systems
  • 24/7 support for critical medical operations

Value Proposition

  • Addresses the critical gap of non-existent sysadmins in GP centers
  • Ensures reliable, secure IT infrastructure
  • Enables seamless integration with existing systems
3

Medical Software Update Management

Proactive management and updating of medical sector-specific software to ensure latest versions are available and installed

Key Features

  • Automated monitoring of software update availability
  • Planned update schedules that work around medical center operations
  • Testing environment validation before production deployment
  • Rollback capabilities if issues arise

Value Proposition

  • Addresses the common issue where medical centers don't bother with available software upgrades
  • Ensures access to latest features, security patches, and improvements
  • Reduces security risks from outdated software
4

Communication Tools

Secure, integrated communication platforms for team and patient interactions

Components

  • Internal chat services for secure team communication
  • Call services for patient and team interactions
  • Email services
  • Integrated communication platforms

Key Features

  • Secure, GDPR-compliant communication
  • Integration with existing workflows
  • Support for remote and hybrid work models
5

Productivity Tools

Office suite and collaboration tools for medical professionals

Components

  • Office suite solutions
  • Document management
  • Collaboration tools

Value Proposition

  • Replaces fragmented software solutions
  • Enables seamless document sharing and collaboration
  • Supports remote work workflows
6

Workforce Organization Tools

Comprehensive workforce planning and management system

Components

  • Shift planning and scheduling systems
  • Vacation and sick-leave management
  • Workforce coordination and optimization
  • Nurse-to-doctor ratio optimization
  • Team composition management
  • Real-time visibility into who is working and with whom

Value Proposition

  • Replaces Excel-based planning systems
  • Provides data-driven workforce optimization
  • Ensures proper staffing ratios and team composition
7

Organizational Structure & Design

Strategic organizational structure design and optimization for medical centers facing professional shortages

Components

  • Organizational structure analysis and design
  • Work planning optimization for limited professional resources
  • Role definition and responsibility mapping
  • Team structure recommendations
  • Workflow design to maximize efficiency

Value Proposition

  • Addresses the critical challenge of lack of professionals
  • Enables effective work planning despite staff shortages
  • Optimizes organizational structure to work with available resources
8

Satisfaction Tracking & Workload Monitoring

Comprehensive system for tracking patient satisfaction and monitoring workload of doctors and nurses to prevent overwhelm

Components

  • Patient satisfaction tracking and analytics
  • Social rating monitoring and management
  • Workload tracking for doctors and nurses
  • Real-time workload visibility and alerts
  • Overwhelm prevention and early warning systems

Value Proposition

  • Addresses the issue of poor social ratings for family centers
  • Enables tracking of actual workload to understand capacity constraints
  • Prevents doctors and nurses from becoming overwhelmed
