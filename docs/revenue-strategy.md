# Revenue Models & Offerings

## Proven Revenue Models (You Can Combine These)

### 1. Per-Staff-Member Monthly Subscription (Main Engine)

This should be your primary model.

**Example Pricing (illustrative, not final):**
- €25–€60 per active staff member / month
- Scales with clinic size
- Predictable recurring revenue

---

### 2. Hardware-as-a-Service (High Margin Over Time)

Start as a middleman → transition to ownership.

**Model:**
- €40–€90 / device / month
- Includes:
  - Laptop
  - Headset
  - Setup
  - Replacement
  - Remote support

**Why Hospitals Love This:**
- No upfront CAPEX
- No inventory management
- No IT headaches

**Your Profit:**
- Early: 10–20% margin as reseller
- Later: 40–60% margin once you own hardware

This becomes sticky revenue.

---

### 3. "Operational Rescue" Projects (High-Ticket Entry)

This is how you land your first clients.

**One-Time Fees:**
- €5k–€50k per engagement

**Examples:**
- "Clinic digitization in 30 days"
- "Staff shortage operational redesign"
- "Pre-audit stabilization"

**You:**
- Map workflows
- Fix communication
- Redesign shifts
- Install your system

-> Then convert them to monthly subscription

---

Let's translate your list into things they'll pay for:

### 2. IT Support

### 4. Communication

**Revenue:** Bundled
**Upsell:** Compliance logging, call recording (where legal)

### 5. Office Tools

### 6. Workforce Management

### 8. Satisfaction & Workload Tracking
