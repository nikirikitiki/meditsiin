# Core Idea

Build a medical center modernization service.

## The Problem

Estonian GP centers (Family Centers) face:
- **Technology issues:**
    - Outdated computers
    - Outdated medical software versions
    - Fragmented tools (messaging, patient communication, shift planning, vacation/sick leave tracking)

- **Organizational issues:**
    - Nurse and doctor shortages
    - No IT support (hardware and software management)

- **Patient & staff satisfaction:**
    - Poor online ratings
    - No workload tracking
    - No staff satisfaction tracking

- **Modernization barriers:**
    - Can't enable remote work
    - Staff frustrated with old, slow technology

## What We're Building

A bundle that gives GP centers everything they need to modernize:

1. **Hardware** - Rent laptops and headphones (start as middleman, later own hardware)
2. **IT Support** - System administration (they have zero, we provide it)
3. **VPN Service** - Secure remote access for medical data and systems
4. **Communication** - Chat, calls, email (integrate existing platforms)
5. **Office Tools** - Office suite, document management
6. **Workforce Management** - Shift planning, vacation/sick leave, nurse-to-doctor ratios, who's working with whom
7. **Organizational Design** - Help structure teams when they're short on professionals
8. **Satisfaction & Workload Tracking** - Monitor patient satisfaction and prevent staff overwhelm

And this all to enable remote work.

## Our Goal

Modernize GP centers and make them more appealing to young doctors. Enabling remote work reduces the burden of being a doctor.

Long-term: Build a network of doctors for freelance work at medical centers during shortages.

Future expansion: Expand to neighbouring countries with similar GP systems (i.e. Finland).


**By Spring 2026:**
- Core platform ready (hardware rental, IT support, VPN, communication, workforce tools, organizational design, satisfaction tracking)
- Start direct sales

**Later (2-3 years):**
- Analytics platform
- Blood pressure monitoring app (patients send photos of BP monitor, doctors see graphs)
- LLM tool for processing patient data (format, extract important info)

**Much Later (3-5+ years):**
- Own hardware instead of middleman
- Flexible staffing pool (network of doctors for daily contracts)
- Expand to Finland

## Business Model

Subscription per workplace:
- **Workplace with computer**: Hardware (laptop, headphones) + all software/services
- **Workplace without computer**: Software only (for remote workers using own devices)

Single unified bill. Customer chooses number of workplaces and mix.

## Why This Works

- No one else bundles all this together
- They need it (old tech, no IT support)
- One bill instead of managing 5+ vendors
- Enables remote work (attracts young professionals)
- Solves real pain points

## Core Principles

- Integrate existing platforms, don't build everything from scratch
- GDPR compliant (EU requirements)
- Patient safety first
- Focus on core functionality, add analytics/advanced stuff later
- Direct sales initially, partnerships later
