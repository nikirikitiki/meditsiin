# Goals

## Strategic Goals

1. **Become the trusted digitalization partner** for medical establishments seeking to enable remote work capabilities
2. **Build a comprehensive platform** that addresses hardware, communication, and workforce management needs
3. **Establish credibility** in the high-stakes medical field through reliable, secure, and effective solutions
4. **Create a sustainable business model** that supports long-term growth and innovation
5. **Develop a network** of medical professionals and establishments that benefit from our solutions

---

## Short-Term Goals

### Phase 1: Foundation & Development (Now - Spring 2026)
- **Platform Development:**
  - Research and create shortlist of existing platforms for integration
  - Integrate communication tools (internal chat, call services, email)
  - Integrate office suite solutions
  - Develop workforce organization tools (shift planning, vacation/sick-leave management)
  - Build advanced workforce planning features (nurse-to-doctor ratios, team composition)
  
- **Infrastructure Setup:**
  - Establish hardware rental capabilities (laptops, headphones)
  - Set up system administration service offerings
  - Ensure GDPR and EU compliance across all solutions
  - Build integration capabilities with existing medical software

- **Business Development:**
  - Finalize subscription pricing model (per-seat: doctor, nurse, administrator)
  - Develop sales materials and value propositions
  - Prepare for direct sales approach
  - Build brand recognition in Estonian GP center community

- **Team & Operations:**
  - Leverage expertise of founding team (1 medical professional, 1 IT solutions architect)
  - Establish operational processes and support systems
  - Set up customer support infrastructure

- **Milestone: Spring 2026**
  - Have offering ready to some extent
  - Begin visiting customers and direct sales efforts

### Phase 2: Launch & Growth (Spring 2026 - 2027)
- Acquire initial GP center clients in Estonia
- Refine and improve core offerings based on client feedback
- Expand client base (target: GP centers with <50 employees)
- Develop partnerships with medical establishments
- Scale operations to support multiple clients
- Establish customer success and support infrastructure
- Transition from direct sales to include partnerships and referrals

---

## Long-Term Goals

### Phase 3: Analytics & Insights (2-3 years)
- Launch analytics platform for team wellbeing monitoring
- Develop efficiency metrics and reporting tools
- Provide data-driven insights to medical establishments
- Build predictive analytics capabilities
- Create dashboards and reporting systems
- Integrate analytics with workforce planning tools

### Phase 4: Proprietary Hardware Solutions (2-4 years)
- Develop own hardware solutions specifically designed for medical centers
- Launch hardware rental services for clinics
- Create customized medical workstations
- Build hardware-as-a-service offering

### Phase 5: Flexible Staffing Network (3-5+ years)
- Create and manage a pool of qualified doctors available on daily contract basis
- Develop on-demand staffing platform
- Enable daily contract basis for doctors
- Integrate staffing solutions with existing workforce management tools
- Build a network effect that benefits both medical establishments and practitioners

### Phase 6: Geographic Expansion to Finland (3-5+ years)
- Expand operations to Finland
- Adapt solutions for Finnish GP centers and healthcare system
- Establish presence in Finnish market
- Build partnerships with Finnish medical establishments
- Leverage learnings from Estonian market to accelerate Finnish expansion
- Ensure compliance with Finnish regulations and requirements

---

## Key Objectives

### Operational Objectives
- Ensure 99.9% uptime for all communication and workforce management tools
- Maintain GDPR and EU compliance standards for all medical data
- Provide responsive system administration support
- Deliver seamless integration with existing medical software systems
- Support GP centers in Estonia (primary market)
- Handle hardware rental and maintenance efficiently

### Business Objectives
- Achieve sustainable revenue growth through subscription model
- Build a strong customer base of Estonian GP centers (target: <50 employees)
- Develop recurring revenue streams with per-seat pricing (doctor, nurse, administrator)
- Create strategic partnerships in the Estonian healthcare ecosystem
- Transition from direct sales to partnership/referral model
- Establish market leadership in Estonian GP center digitalization
- Expand to Finland as a strategic second market (long-term)

### Product Objectives
- Continuously improve user experience based on feedback from medical professionals
- Expand feature set to meet evolving needs of GP centers
- Maintain competitive advantage through integrated, comprehensive solutions
- Ensure scalability of all solutions
- Successfully integrate with existing platforms (shortlist to be developed)
- Build robust workforce planning tools that replace Excel-based solutions

## Success Metrics

### Customer Metrics
- Number of GP centers (Family Centers) using our platform in Estonia
- Number of GP centers using our platform in Finland (long-term)
- Customer retention rate
- Net Promoter Score (NPS)
- Customer satisfaction scores
- Number of active medical practitioners on the platform (doctors, nurses, administrators)
- Percentage of GP centers in Estonia using our solution
- Market penetration in Finland (long-term)

### Product Metrics
- Platform uptime and reliability
- User adoption rates for each tool
- Feature utilization rates
- System performance metrics
- Security incident rate (target: zero)

### Business Metrics
- Monthly Recurring Revenue (MRR)
- Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC)
- Lifetime Value (LTV)
- Revenue growth rate
- Market share in target segments

### Impact Metrics
- Percentage of medical practitioners enabled to work remotely
- Reduction in operational inefficiencies for clients
- Improvement in team wellbeing scores (when analytics available)
- Number of successful staffing placements (when staffing pool available)
